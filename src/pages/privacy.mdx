# 隱私權政策

**生效日期：** 2026 年 10 月 11 日

本政策說明探索印記（ExploreStamp，以下稱「我們」）的 iOS／Android App 與官網 www.explorestamp.com 如何處理你的資料。

## 1. 摘要

- App 預設不用登入即可使用，我們不收集姓名、Email、電話等個人資料。
- 打卡紀錄、照片與備註儲存在你的裝置上，不會上傳到我們或任何第三方的伺服器。
- 位置只在你按下「蓋章打卡」或開啟地圖時使用，不在背景追蹤。

## 2. App 在你的裝置上儲存的資料

| 資料 | 用途 | 儲存位置 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 打卡紀錄（景點、時間、打卡當下的座標與距離） | 印章冊、任務進度、月曆 | 裝置本機資料庫 |
| 你選擇加入的照片與備註 | 月曆封面、造訪紀錄、明信片 | App 專屬儲存空間 |
| 語言、距離單位、提醒時間等設定 | 個人化設定 | 裝置本機 |

這些資料不會離開你的裝置，除非你主動使用「匯出備份」或「分享明信片」，並自行選擇傳送的對象或儲存位置。

## 3. 權限

| 權限 | 用途 | 拒絕時的影響 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 定位（僅使用期間） | 確認你位於景點打卡範圍內；在地圖上顯示你的位置與距離 | 無法打卡，地圖仍可瀏覽 |
| 相機 | 打卡時拍照 | 可改從相簿選取，或不附照片 |
| 相簿 | 選取既有照片 | 可改用相機，或不附照片 |
| 通知 | 景點推薦提醒（預設關閉） | 不會收到提醒 |

App 不會在背景存取你的位置。提醒通知由裝置在本機排程，不經過伺服器。

## 4. App 的網路連線

App 只會為以下用途連線，且不會在連線中附帶你的打卡紀錄、照片或位置：

- **景點目錄更新**：向 api.explorestamp.com 查詢是否有新版景點資料與任務集封面圖片。
- **地圖圖磚**：向 OpenStreetMap 下載地圖圖片。如同一般網路請求，對方伺服器會看到你的 IP 位址，請參考 [OpenStreetMap 基金會隱私權政策](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy)。

App 未內建任何廣告、分析或追蹤 SDK。

## 5. 官網

官網使用 Google Analytics 與 Google Tag Manager 統計瀏覽量與使用情形，可能透過 Cookie 收集瀏覽器類型、造訪頁面與大致地區等匿名資訊。你可以在瀏覽器設定中封鎖 Cookie。官網的電子報訂閱由 Substack 提供，訂閱時填寫的 Email 依 Substack 的隱私權政策處理。

## 6. 資料分享

我們不會出售、出租或分享你的資料。由於使用者資料只存在你的裝置上，我們本身也無法存取。

## 7. 你的控制權

- 在 App「設定」中可以匯出備份、匯入備份，或清除所有打卡紀錄與照片。
- 刪除 App 會一併移除所有本機資料。刪除前若想保留紀錄，請先匯出備份。
- 可隨時在系統設定中撤銷定位、相機、相簿與通知權限。

## 8. 兒童隱私

App 不收集任何個人資料，也不針對 13 歲以下兒童蒐集資訊。

## 9. 政策變更

未來若推出會員登入或雲端備份等選用功能，我們會在推出前更新本政策，說明會收集哪些資料及其用途，並由你自行決定是否啟用。不登入的使用方式會繼續維持本政策所述的做法。

政策若有其他更新，會公布於本頁並修改生效日期。

## 10. 聯絡我們

對本政策有任何疑問，請來信至[聯絡信箱](mailto:explorestamp@gmail.com)。

# Privacy Policy (English)

**Effective date:** October 11, 2026

This policy explains how the ExploreStamp iOS/Android app and the website www.explorestamp.com handle your data.

## Summary

- The app works without signing in. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number or other personal information.
- Check-ins, photos and notes are stored on your device and are not uploaded to our servers or any third party.
- Your location is used only when you tap "Check in" or open the map. The app never tracks your location in the background.

## Data stored on your device

- **Check-ins** (spot, time, your coordinates and distance at check-in): used for the stamp book, collection progress and calendar.
- **Photos and notes you choose to add**: used for the calendar, visit history and postcards.
- **Settings** such as language, distance unit and reminder time.

This data leaves your device only when you choose to export a backup or share a postcard, and you decide where it goes.

## Permissions

- **Location (while using the app):** to confirm you are within a spot's check-in radius and to show your position on the map. Without it you cannot check in, but you can still browse the map.
- **Camera / Photo library:** to attach photos to a check-in. Either one can be used instead of the other, and photos are optional.
- **Notifications:** for optional spot reminders (off by default). Reminders are scheduled locally on your device.

## Network access

The app connects to the network only to:

- check api.explorestamp.com for catalog updates and collection cover images;
- load map tiles from OpenStreetMap, which receives your IP address as part of any web request (see the [OSMF Privacy Policy](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy)).

No check-ins, photos or location data are included in these requests. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.

## Website

The website uses Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager to measure traffic, which may collect anonymous information such as browser type, pages visited and approximate region through cookies. Newsletter sign-ups are handled by Substack under its own privacy policy.

## Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data. Because your data stays on your device, we have no access to it.

## Your choices

- Export, import or erase all check-ins and photos from the app's Settings.
- Deleting the app removes all local data. Export a backup first if you want to keep it.
- Revoke location, camera, photo and notification permissions at any time in system settings.

## Children

The app collects no personal information and does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## Changes

If we introduce optional features such as sign-in or cloud backup, we will update this policy before they launch, explaining what data is collected and why. These features will be opt-in, and using the app without signing in will continue to work as described here.

Other updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

## Contact

Questions about this policy? Please reach us via our [contact email](mailto:explorestamp@gmail.com).
