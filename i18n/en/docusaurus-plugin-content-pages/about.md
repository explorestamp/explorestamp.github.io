# About ExploreStamp

ExploreStamp is an iOS and Android app that brings the habit of collecting stamps onto your phone.

In Taiwan, many railway stations, museums and tourist spots keep a commemorative stamp, and plenty of people carry a little booklet full of them. We wanted to keep what makes this fun — you have to actually go there to get that stamp — and add what paper can't do: photos, dates, a map, and routes planned in advance.

## What the app does

**Themed collections**: places are grouped into routes by theme, such as "6 Classic Taiwan Railway Stations", "A Walk Through Tainan" and "Night Markets & Street Food". Each route shows your progress.

**Location check-in**: tap "Check in" at a place, and the app confirms you're within its check-in range. Once you are, the stamp goes into your stamp book. The range depends on the type of place, usually between 150 and 500 meters.

**Stamp book**: every place has its own stamp. The frame shape shows the category, and the stamp carries the place's name and the date of your first visit. Places you haven't been to yet appear as dashed outlines.

**Calendar**: every visit is saved to your calendar, with a photo from that day as the cover. Each check-in can include up to 6 photos and a short note.

**Postcards**: combine a photo from the day with the stamp, the place name and the date into a postcard, and send it through your phone's share sheet.

**Map, reminders and backup**: see every place and your check-in status on a map; set daily or weekend reminders that suggest a place; export your records and photos to a backup file and import it on a new phone.

## What's included

The app currently includes 43 places in Taiwan, 30 in Japan (including Okinawa) and 8 in Macao, in seven categories: museums and arts, historic sites, libraries, railway stations, factory tours, local food, and parks and nature. The catalog updates automatically in the background, with no need to reinstall.

See the full list under [Places](/explore).

## Privacy

ExploreStamp works without signing in. Your location is read once, only when you tap check in, and your check-ins and photos are stored on your phone and aren't uploaded by default. If accounts and cloud backup are added in the future, they will be optional features you turn on yourself. For details, see the [privacy policy](/privacy).
