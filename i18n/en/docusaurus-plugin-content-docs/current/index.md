## Check-in range

When you tap "Check in" at a place, the app checks how far you are from it. The default range for each category is below. Places that cover a large area, such as night markets and art districts, have their own settings.

| Category | Check-in range |
| --- | --- |
| Parks & Nature | 500 m |
| Factory Tours | 400 m |
| Museums & Arts, Historic Sites | 300 m |
| Railway Stations | 250 m |
| Libraries | 200 m |
| Local Food | 150 m |
