# Explore

ExploreStamp currently includes 257 places: 45 in Taiwan, 32 in Japan (including Okinawa), 21 in South Korea, 13 in Hong Kong, 8 in Macao, 22 in Australia, 12 in New Zealand, 13 in Poland, 16 in the United Kingdom, 20 in the United States, 12 in Canada, 10 in Egypt, 13 in Norway, 10 in Sweden and 10 in Finland, grouped into 20 categories and organized into 47 themed routes. The full list of places and their maps are in the app, where the catalog updates automatically in the background.

## Check-in range

When you tap "Check in" at a place, the app checks how far you are from it. The default range for each category is below. Places that cover a large area, such as night markets and art districts, have their own settings.

| Category | Check-in range |
| --- | --- |
| Airports | 1000 m |
| Theme Parks, Nature | 500 m |
| Zoos & Aquariums, University Campuses, Factory Tours | 400 m |
| Museums, Art Museums, Performance & Sports Venues, Landmarks & Viewpoints, Heritage Sites, Places of Worship, Shopping | 300 m |
| Markets, Night Markets, Railway Stations | 250 m |
| Libraries | 200 m |
| Bookstores, Food, Cafés | 150 m |
